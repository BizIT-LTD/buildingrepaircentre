Commercial services

Building Repair Centre takes on large or small commercial works involving all parts of the building scope, including fit-outs, refurbishments and ongoing maintenance of commercial premises.

We know choosing a builder is not easy, so we keep the process simple: tell us about the job, we provide a quote, and we coordinate the trades once you go ahead.

Please contact us to talk about starting a commercial project.

Questions about commercial

Can you work around a business that is still trading?

Tell us your trading hours and any restrictions when you request a quote, and we will plan the work around them where possible.

Do you take on small commercial jobs?

Yes. We take on large and small commercial works, including one-off repairs and ongoing maintenance.

How do I get a quote?

Call us on 0431 848 619 or send an enquiry. Include the address or suburb, a description of the work and, if you can, a few photos.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. For some jobs we will need to inspect the property before we can quote.
