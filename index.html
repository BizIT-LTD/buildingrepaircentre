About Building Repair Centre

Building works and maintenance, organised for you

Building Repair Centre carries out building works and maintenance across Sydney, including drywall and plastering, painting, plumbing, electrical work, carpentry and joinery, rendering and bricklaying.

We work with strata and property managers, insurance building clients, builders and commercial property owners, as well as homeowners who need repairs, maintenance or renovation work.
