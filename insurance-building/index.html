Insurance Building services

When a property is damaged, the first job is to make it safe and stop further damage. After that, the damaged areas need to be repaired and the building returned to its original condition.

Building Repair Centre provides building services for insurance work: from the initial make-safe through to the repairs, using the trades the job needs.

What we do

  • Emergency make safeSecuring and weatherproofing a damaged property, such as temporary covers, propping or boarding up, so it does not get worse while repairs are arranged.
  • Building maintenanceOngoing building maintenance for property owners and managers.
  • All repair jobsRepairs to roofs, ceilings, walls, floors, doors, windows, fences and finishes after damage.
  • All trade servicesCarpentry, plastering, painting, roofing, electrical, plumbing, tiling and the other trades a repair can need.

Questions about insurance building

What should I do straight after storm or tree damage?

Make sure everyone is safe first. If life or property is at immediate risk, call the NSW SES on 132 500 (or 000 in a life-threatening emergency). Take photos of the damage for your insurer, then call us on 0431 848 619 during business hours to arrange make-safe work and repairs.

Do I need to contact my insurer first?

If you are making an insurance claim, contact your insurer as early as possible and follow their instructions. They may need photos, a report or a quote before repairs start.

How do I get a quote?

Call us on 0431 848 619 or send an enquiry. Include the address or suburb, a description of the work and, if you can, a few photos.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. For some jobs we will need to inspect the property before we can quote.
