Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about our services. Can't find what you need? Call us or send an enquiry.

General

What services do you offer?

We offer insurance building, strata and real estate maintenance, commercial and residential building work, plus trade services such as carpentry, painting, plastering, electrical, plumbing and tiling.

What do you specialise in?

We specialise in insurance and renovation repair work. We also take on residential and commercial building work.

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

Which areas do you service?

We service the Sydney region. Tell us the suburb when you request a quote.

Who do you work for?

We provide services to insurance builders, strata and building managers, real estate property managers, builders, colleges, schools and universities, and commercial and residential property owners, on private and government projects.

How do I get a quote?

Call us on 0431 848 619 or send an enquiry. Include the address or suburb, a description of the work and, if you can, a few photos.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. For some jobs we will need to inspect the property before we can quote.

Can you help after storm or tree damage?

Yes. We carry out make-safe work and repairs. If life or property is at immediate risk, call the NSW SES on 132 500, or 000 in a life-threatening emergency.

Do you accept trades and suppliers?

Yes. We accept trades, suppliers and individual tradespeople. Email info@buildingrepaircentre.com.au with your details.

Insurance Building

What should I do straight after storm or tree damage?

Make sure everyone is safe first. If life or property is at immediate risk, call the NSW SES on 132 500 (or 000 in a life-threatening emergency). Take photos of the damage for your insurer, then call us on 0431 848 619 during business hours to arrange make-safe work and repairs.

Do I need to contact my insurer first?

If you are making an insurance claim, contact your insurer as early as possible and follow their instructions. They may need photos, a report or a quote before repairs start.

Strata & Real Estate Maintenance

Can you arrange access with tenants or residents?

Yes. Tell us who the contact is for access, such as the tenant, building manager or owner, and we will arrange a time that suits them.

Do you quote for strata and agency work?

Yes. Send us the work order or a description of the job, the address and any photos, and we will provide a quote.

Commercial

Can you work around a business that is still trading?

Tell us your trading hours and any restrictions when you request a quote, and we will plan the work around them where possible.

Do you take on small commercial jobs?

Yes. We take on large and small commercial works, including one-off repairs and ongoing maintenance.

Residential

Can you do a temporary repair first?

Yes. Depending on what you need, we can carry out a temporary repair to make the area safe, or restore it to its original state.

Do you build decks and pergolas?

Yes. Decks and pergolas are part of our residential work. See the projects page for photos of deck and pergola builds.
